Before you start

The questions people ask us first.

How does the 60-minute recording session actually work?

We schedule a comfortable 1-on-1 recording call via Google Meet or Zoom. Our producer leads the conversation like an engaging podcast, asking strategic, pre-researched questions about your industry, experiences, and perspectives. You don't need to write scripts, prepare slides, or rehearse—just show up and talk naturally about what you know.

What exact deliverables make up the 36 monthly pieces of content?

From your single 60-minute interview, our post-production team extracts and edits 36 turnkey assets:

  • 8 Short-Form Videos: 9:16 vertical edits with dynamic hooks, kinetic subtitles, and pacing for Shorts, Reels & TikTok.
  • 8 Custom Written Posts: Long-form insights, hooks, and formatted copy ready for LinkedIn & X.
  • 20 Branded Quote Cards: High-impact visual graphics designed with your custom brand colors & typography.
  • 30-Day Publishing Calendar: An organized schedule laid out so you always know what to post next.

How fast is the turnaround, and when do I get my first batch of clips?

You will receive your first batch of edited, ready-to-publish short-form clips within 72 hours of completing your interview call. All deliverables are uploaded directly to your private Client Portal where you can preview, download, or request adjustments.

Which social media platforms are the videos and graphics formatted for?

Every piece is natively tailored and formatted for the top 5 high-converting platforms: LinkedIn, Instagram (Reels), YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and X (Twitter). We provide the exact vertical aspect ratios (9:16), graphic sizes, and platform-specific caption copy with hashtags.

Can I request revisions if I want tweaks or pacing adjustments?

Yes, absolutely. We want you to feel 100% proud of every piece before it goes live. You can request revisions directly through your Client Portal, and our editing team will fine-tune the hooks, cuts, subtitles, or captions until it matches your exact authentic voice.

Do I need a professional camera, studio microphone, or lighting?

Not at all. Over 90% of our clients record using their standard laptop webcam, iPhone/Android, or external USB webcam paired with everyday AirPods or standard earbuds in a quiet room. Our post-production team handles the audio cleanup, dynamic camera zooming, color correction, and animated graphics.

How does billing work, and can I pause or cancel anytime?

Clipora Monthly operates on a flexible month-to-month subscription with zero long-term contracts. You can pause your subscription, skip a month, or cancel anytime with one click in your billing settings. You maintain 100% permanent commercial ownership of all raw footage and edited content.

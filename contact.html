Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking an introductory session with Clipora.

Do I need to prepare a script or outline before the call?
Not at all. You don't need any slides, written scripts, or elaborate notes. Just show up as you are. We'll ask you a few natural questions about what you do, what problems you solve for your clients, and what you see differently from others in your industry.
Is this call a high-pressure sales pitch?
Zero pressure. We use this 15-minute call to evaluate whether our interview-led content model is genuinely a good fit for your business and goals. If it's not a match, we'll tell you honestly and point you toward alternatives.
What equipment do I need for the actual recording session?
You don't need a professional TV studio. A modern laptop webcam (or smartphone) and a quiet room with decent lighting and standard earbuds/AirPods are all it takes. Our video post-production team handles high-definition grading, dynamic framing, background sound enhancement, and custom graphics.
Can I jump straight to recording if I'm already ready?
Yes! If you already know you want to start, you can skip the intro call and sign up directly on our checkout page. Once you submit, you'll receive our private booking calendar to schedule your full 60-minute interview recording immediately.
What if I need to reschedule or cancel my call?
Every calendar confirmation email includes a direct 1-click reschedule link. You can change your date or time anytime up to 1 hour before the scheduled session at no cost.
